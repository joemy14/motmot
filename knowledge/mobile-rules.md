# Mobile rules for kids games

- Buttons: at least 25% of screen width and 10% of screen height.
- Labels: at most 4 words. Use icons when possible.
- Main action buttons go at the bottom center, within thumb reach.
- Maximum 6 buttons on one screen.
- Every tap gives feedback: a squash animation and a sound.
- Bright colors, high contrast, dark outline.
- No chat, no outside links, no countdown timers that push players to buy.
